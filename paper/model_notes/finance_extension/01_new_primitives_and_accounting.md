# New Primitives and Accounting

Developer type is \(\theta_i=(a_i,k_i,\ell_i)\sim H(a,k,\ell)\), with arbitrary
correlation. The three coordinates respectively measure project-advancement
capability, internal manufacturing capability, and predetermined capacity to
finance commercialization commitments.

Internal organization requires \(J_I(m,k_i)>0\), with
\(J_{I,m}>0\) and \(J_{I,k}<0\). Entrusted organization requires
\(J_E(m)>0\), with \(J_E'(m)>0\). The locally relevant financing advantage is
\(J_E(m)<J_I(m,k_i)\); it is not imposed globally.

Accounting convention:

- \(F_I\) is the real internal readiness/setup cost and is subtracted once;
- \(J_I\) is a financeability threshold and is never subtracted;
- \(F_E,p_mb(m),\mu_E,\tau_E\) each enter entrusted value once;
- \(J_E\) is a financeability threshold and is never subtracted.

The old convention \(F_I=+\infty\) below a capability cutoff is removed.
Low \(k_i\) raises the finite setup cost and financing requirement; sufficient
\(\ell_i\) can therefore restore internalization.
