# 06 Empirical Promotion Criteria

Status: complete

The baseline empirical ordering is:

1. estimate the MAH effect on holder--manufacturer separation and downstream
   project/product outcomes;
2. test heterogeneity by predetermined internal manufacturing capability;
3. add financing measures only as secondary heterogeneity.

Finance proxies must be pre-policy and separated from size, age, ownership,
business-group affiliation, listed status, and pre-policy innovation history.
Financing may be promoted only if it independently predicts the post-MAH
holder--manufacturer separation margin after these controls, matches an
extension prediction, and receives new user authorization.

Clinical-trial and patent outcomes do not by themselves validate the finance
mechanism. Patents remain outside the baseline. Gu (2024) is treated as an
empirical boundary on clinical development and patents, while the paper's
distinct primary margin is regulatory separation and commercialization
organization.
