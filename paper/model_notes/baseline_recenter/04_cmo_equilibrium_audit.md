# 04 CMO Equilibrium Audit

Status: complete

Study demand integrates over $H(a,k)$ and contains two endogenous responses to
a candidate CMO price: entrusted-route selection and common project
advancement through $\Omega_i(M,p_m)$. No financing indicator enters either
response.

Under zero-mass route ties, continuity, integrability, upward-sloping supply,
and weakly decreasing demand, the scalar clearing equation has the maintained
existence and uniqueness properties. The reform can raise the equilibrium CMO
price when it creates positive entrusted demand, and this price response
weakly attenuates the fixed-price value and advancement gains.

The observed-outcome timing remains: anticipated route availability affects
$x_i$; project draws and route assignment occur later; holder--manufacturer
separation is then observed; realized products follow under exogenous
$s(q)$ or $s_g(q)$.
