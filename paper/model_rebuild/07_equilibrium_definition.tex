\section{Baseline MAH Partial Equilibrium}
\label{sec:p07-equilibrium}

Fix the institutional regime \(M\in\{0,1\}\), the developer distribution
\(H(a,k)\), the project distribution \(F(q,m)\), the qualified-supplier
distribution \(H_C(z)\), and all other exogenous primitives introduced in
Phases~1--6. A baseline MAH partial equilibrium is the collection
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{
  \left\{
    p_m^*,\;
    x_i^*,\;
    r_i^*(q,m),\;
    s_j^*
  \right\}_{i,j}}
  .
  \label{eq:p07-equilibrium-collection}
\end{equation}
The suppressed arguments are \(x_i^*=x_i^*(M,p_m^*)\),
\(r_i^*(q,m)=r_i^*(q,m;M,p_m^*)\), and
\(s_j^*=s_j^*(p_m^*,z_j)\). The collection contains exactly these four
families of endogenous optimal objects.

It satisfies the following four conditions.

\paragraph{1. Optimal commercialization route.}
For every planning-stage project--developer pair outside the zero-measure tie
set,
\begin{equation}
  r_i^*(q,m;M,p_m^*)
  \in
  \arg\max_{r\in\{I,E,T,A\}}
  \left\{
    W_i^I(q,m),
    W_i^E(q,m;M,p_m^*),
    W^T(q,m),
    W^A
  \right\},
  \label{eq:p07-route-optimality}
\end{equation}
with route \(E\) unavailable when \(M=0\). This is the deterministic Phase~4
choice; route \(E\) retains holder rights and is not route \(T\).

\paragraph{2. Optimal project advancement.}
For every developer \(i\), the common project-advancement control is
\begin{equation}
  x_i^*(M,p_m^*)
  =
  \left[
    \frac{\beta a_i\Omega_i(M,p_m^*)}{\kappa}
  \right]^{1/\nu},
  \label{eq:p07-advancement-optimality}
\end{equation}
including the zero-value corner \(x_i^*=0\). There is no class-specific
\(x_{ig}\) control.

\paragraph{3. Optimal qualified-supplier capacity.}
For every qualified supplier \(j\),
\begin{equation}
  s_j^*(p_m^*,z_j)
  \in
  \arg\max_{s_j\geq0}
  \left\{
    p_m^*s_j-\Psi(s_j;z_j)
  \right\}.
  \label{eq:p07-capacity-optimality}
\end{equation}
Strict convexity of \(\Psi\) makes this capacity choice unique. Supplier entry
is not a margin of the model.

\paragraph{4. Qualified-capacity market clearing.}
The price \(p_m^*\) is the unique solution to
\begin{equation}
  D_m(p_m^*;M)=S_m(p_m^*).
  \label{eq:p07-market-clearing}
\end{equation}
The Phase~6 existence and uniqueness conditions apply. The demand and supply
functions determine the price but are not additional members of the
equilibrium collection.

\subsection{Closure boundary}
\label{subsec:p07-closure-boundary}

Equations~\eqref{eq:p07-route-optimality}--\eqref{eq:p07-market-clearing}
close only commercialization route choice, common project advancement,
qualified-supplier capacity, and the manufacturing-service price. There is no
entry condition and no labor-market, capital-market, product-market aggregate,
firm-distribution, or welfare-clearing condition. Product price \(p^*(c)\)
remains a conditional firm-level optimum from Phase~2, not a second
market-clearing price.
