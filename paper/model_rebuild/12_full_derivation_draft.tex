\begin{abstract}
This document assembles the approved model-rebuild modules and the subsequent
financing revision for commercialization. The baseline has one binary
institutional wedge, predetermined financing capacity used only to screen
organizational feasibility, deterministic route choice, one common
project-advancement control, and one qualified-capacity market. The reform
does not increase financing supply. Research--development allocation and
patent production remain outside the active model.
\end{abstract}
