\begin{abstract}
This document assembles the approved model-rebuild modules around a
commercialization-organization baseline. The baseline has one binary
institutional wedge, developer heterogeneity in project advancement and
internal manufacturing capability, deterministic route choice, one common
project-advancement control, and one qualified-capacity market. Explicit
financing constraints are retained only in an inactive extension.
Research--development allocation and patent production remain outside the
active model.
\end{abstract}
