\section{Extension: Financing the Commercialization Organization}
\label{sec:ext-financing-commercialization}

\noindent\textbf{Not part of the baseline.}
The baseline captures the cost of weak manufacturing capability through
\(c_I(m,k_i)\) and \(F_I(m,k_i)\). This extension asks whether imperfect
finance additionally prevents a developer from funding an otherwise valuable
commercialization organization. It does not change the MAH policy channel,
introduce a credit-supply response, or alter the baseline advancement control.

\subsection{Financing capacity and liquidity requirements}

Augment the developer state only inside this extension to
\begin{equation}
  \theta_i^F=(a_i,k_i,\ell_i),
  \qquad \ell_i>0,
  \label{eq:ext-finance-type}
\end{equation}
where \(\ell_i\) is internal liquidity plus external finance available under
limited pledgeability before commercialization revenue is realized. Internal
and entrusted organization require peak up-front liquidity
\begin{equation}
  J_I(m,k_i)>0,
  \qquad J_{I,m}>0,
  \qquad J_{I,k}<0,
  \label{eq:ext-internal-liquidity}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
  J_E(m)>0,
  \qquad J_E'(m)>0.
  \label{eq:ext-entrusted-liquidity}
\end{equation}
The objects \(J_I\) and \(J_E\) are liquidity thresholds, not additional
real costs. The route values \(W_i^I\) and \(W_i^E\) already subtract all
real present-value costs through \(F_I,F_E,p_mb(m),\mu_E\), and the operating
cost kernels. The liquidity requirements are therefore never subtracted from
payoffs.

\subsection{Finance-adjusted route choice}

Finance-adjusted retained-route values are
\begin{equation}
  \widetilde W_i^I(q,m;\ell_i)
  =
  \begin{cases}
    W_i^I(q,m),&J_I(m,k_i)\leq\ell_i,\\
    -\infty,&J_I(m,k_i)>\ell_i,
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:ext-finance-adjusted-internal}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
  \widetilde W_i^E(q,m;M,p_m,\ell_i)
  =
  \begin{cases}
    W_i^E(q,m;M,p_m),&J_E(m)\leq\ell_i,\\
    -\infty,&J_E(m)>\ell_i.
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:ext-finance-adjusted-entrusted}
\end{equation}
The extension's optimized value and deterministic route are
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
  \widetilde W_i^F(q,m;M,p_m,\ell_i)
  &=\max\{\widetilde W_i^I,\widetilde W_i^E,W^T,0\},\\
  \widetilde r_i^F(q,m;M,p_m,\ell_i)
  &\in\arg\max_{r\in\{I,E,T,A\}}
  \{\widetilde W_i^I,\widetilde W_i^E,W^T,0\}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:ext-finance-route-choice}
\end{equation}
The superscript \(F\) prevents these extension objects from being mistaken for
the baseline value and route.

\subsection{Financing regions}

For low-capability developers, impose only the local condition
\begin{equation}
  J_E(m)<J_I(m,k_i).
  \label{eq:ext-local-liquidity-ordering}
\end{equation}
It is not a global ranking of entrusted and internal production costs. At a
fixed developer--project pair it produces three financing regions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(\ell_i<J_E(m)\): neither retained route can be funded;
  \item \(J_E(m)\leq\ell_i<J_I(m,k_i)\): retained entrusted production can be
  funded but internalization cannot;
  \item \(\ell_i\geq J_I(m,k_i)\): both retained routes can be funded.
\end{enumerate}
The middle interval is the extension-only \emph{financing corridor}. A strict
MAH gain still requires the financed entrusted value to exceed transfer and
delay. Once internalization is restored as an old option, the reform treatment
effect can shrink, remain positive, or become zero; it therefore has no global
monotone sign in \(\ell_i\). These statements are absent from every baseline
proposition and from the baseline CMO demand system.

\subsection{Nesting and empirical promotion rule}

If
\begin{equation}
  \ell_i\geq\max\{J_I(m,k_i),J_E(m)\}
  \label{eq:ext-no-binding-finance}
\end{equation}
for all relevant developer--project pairs, then
\begin{equation}
  \widetilde W_i^I=W_i^I,
  \qquad
  \widetilde W_i^E=W_i^E,
  \label{eq:ext-finance-nesting}
\end{equation}
and the extension collapses exactly to the organizational baseline.

Empirically, financing capacity must be measured before reform exposure and
distinguished from manufacturing capability, size, age, ownership,
business-group affiliation, listed status, and pre-policy innovation history.
The finance extension should be promoted into the baseline only if such
measures independently predict holder--manufacturer separation after these
controls and display a pattern consistent with the extension's financing
regions. Clinical-trial or patent heterogeneity alone is insufficient.
