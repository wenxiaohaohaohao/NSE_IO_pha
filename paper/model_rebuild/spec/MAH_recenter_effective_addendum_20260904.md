## Binding current model

1. The baseline developer type is $\theta_i=(a_i,k_i)\sim H(a,k)$.
2. $a_i$ is project-advancement capability and $k_i$ is internal
   manufacturing capability.
3. Internal production is available for every $k_i>0$. Weak capability raises
   finite $c_I(m,k_i)$ and $F_I(m,k_i)$; there is no hard positive-$k$ cutoff.
4. The baseline contains one common $x_i$, deterministic $I/E/T/A$ choice,
   and one scalar CMO market.
5. MAH changes only the institutional availability/wedge of retained
   entrusted production. It does not shift financing supply, capabilities,
   project draws, realization, CMO supply, or background demand.
6. Main-text formal results are limited to four propositions:
   organizational sorting, the MAH-relevant set, advancement response, and
   CMO equilibrium/scarcity.
7. Financing capacity $\ell_i$, liquidity thresholds $J_I,J_E$, financeability
   screens, and the financing corridor are confined to an explicitly inactive
   appendix extension.
8. Financing is secondary empirical heterogeneity. Promotion requires
   pre-policy measurement, controls separating it from capability and firm
   scale, an independent prediction for holder--manufacturer separation, and
   new user authorization.
9. The research/development allocation and patent-production module remains
   archived and inactive. Patent outcomes are an empirical boundary, not a
   baseline endogenous outcome.
