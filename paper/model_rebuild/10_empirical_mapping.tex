\section{Empirical Mapping Interface}
\label{sec:p10-mapping}

This section leaves audited insertion points for future data. It does not
claim that any named measure is currently available, complete, validated, or
identifying. Every interface specifies a unit, observational grain, required
join keys, availability status, and identification limit.

\subsection{Developer and project interfaces}
\label{subsec:p10-developer-project}

\subsubsection{Interface 1: Predetermined research capability \texorpdfstring{\(a_i\)}{a(i)}}
\textit{Meaning:} pre-existing ability to generate and advance viable
original-drug projects.
\textit{Candidate measure:} pre-policy development history, innovative-drug
pipeline, scientific labor, or patent history after construct validation.
\textit{Unit:} capability index or pre-policy event rate.
\textit{Grain:} developer by pre-policy period.
\textit{Required keys:} stable developer identifier, source identifier, and
event or reporting date.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED; no source is asserted available.
\textit{Identification limit:} patent history may proxy predetermined
capability, but it is not \(x_i\) and does not identify \(a_i\) separately
without additional structure.

\subsubsection{Interface 2: Advancement intensity \texorpdfstring{\(x_i\)}{x(i)}}
\textit{Meaning:} original-drug innovation investment or
project-advancement intensity.
\textit{Candidate measure:} validated development-stage activity.
\textit{Unit:} latent intensity or investment per developer-period.
\textit{Grain:} developer by decision cohort or period.
\textit{Required keys:} developer identifier and decision-cohort date, with a
documented activity definition.
\textit{Availability:} NOT DIRECTLY OBSERVED and NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} \(x_i\) is neither pure clinical effort nor
patent applications; a candidate measure may combine multiple activities.

\subsubsection{Interface 3: Planning-stage projects \texorpdfstring{\(a_ix_i\)}{a(i)x(i)}}
\textit{Meaning:} viable projects reaching the route-planning stage.
\textit{Candidate measure:} IND or regulatory application activity and early
clinical milestones after a measurement audit.
\textit{Unit:} projects or qualifying events per developer-period.
\textit{Grain:} developer--project--event date.
\textit{Required keys:} developer identifier, project or product identifier,
event type, and event date.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} this is not approval or launch and does not
separately identify \(a_i\) and \(x_i\).

\subsubsection{Interface 4: Internal manufacturing capability \texorpdfstring{\(k_i\)}{k(i)}}
\textit{Meaning:} predetermined ability to manufacture a project's required
technology internally.
\textit{Candidate measure:} pre-reform, project-compatible capacity evidence.
\textit{Unit:} compatibility/capability index or physical capacity in a
defined technology class.
\textit{Grain:} developer--facility--technology class--date.
\textit{Required keys:} developer, facility, technology class, product or
requirement class, and observation date.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} licences or GMP scope alone need not measure
usable project-compatible capacity.

\subsubsection{Interface 5: Project value \texorpdfstring{\(q\)}{project-value index}}
\textit{Meaning:} project commercial/scientific value shifter.
\textit{Candidate measure:} therapeutic value, target value, indication, or
market-size measures after validation.
\textit{Unit:} standardized project-value index.
\textit{Grain:} project--indication--reference date.
\textit{Required keys:} project/product, indication, valuation source, and
reference date.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} proxies discipline a value shifter; they do
not permit a direct MAH shift in \(q\).

\subsubsection{Interface 6: Manufacturing complexity \texorpdfstring{\(m\)}{complexity index}}
\textit{Meaning:} project-specific manufacturing requirement.
\textit{Candidate measure:} modality, dosage form, biologic/sterile status, or
audited technical class.
\textit{Unit:} technology class or validated complexity index.
\textit{Grain:} project/product--manufacturing requirement.
\textit{Required keys:} project/product identifier, modality, dosage form,
technical class, and classification version.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} \(m\) is a project requirement, not developer
capacity and not a policy outcome.

\subsection{Route and realized-outcome interfaces}
\label{subsec:p10-routes-outcomes}

\subsubsection{Interface 7: Retained entrusted route \texorpdfstring{\(r_i^*=E\)}{retained route E}}
\textit{Meaning:} the developer remains the holder while a qualified external
producer manufactures.
\textit{Candidate measure:} product-level holder differs from manufacturer.
\textit{Unit:} binary route indicator.
\textit{Grain:} product--holder--manufacturer--effective date.
\textit{Required keys:} normalized holder identifier, manufacturer identifier,
product identifier, authorization/effective date, and source provenance.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} holder, manufacturer, product and timing must
all align; holder--manufacturer difference is not automatically route \(E\).

\subsubsection{Interface 8: Realized retained outcome \texorpdfstring{\(Y_i^{\mathrm{ret}}\)}{retained outcome}}
\textit{Meaning:} realized innovative product for which the developer retains
holder status.
\textit{Candidate measure:} approval, launch, or marketing-licence outcome
with holder identity.
\textit{Unit:} product event or count.
\textit{Grain:} developer--product--approval/launch date.
\textit{Required keys:} developer/holder, product, event type, event date, and
jurisdiction.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} this is a downstream realized outcome, not a
direct observation of advancement.

\subsubsection{Interface 9: Novelty class \texorpdfstring{\(g\)}{novelty class}}
\textit{Meaning:} exogenous original-versus-incremental empirical classifier.
\textit{Candidate measure:} verified regulatory classification.
\textit{Unit:} categorical label \(O\) or \(\mathrm{Inc}\).
\textit{Grain:} product/project--classification version.
\textit{Required keys:} product/project identifier, classification rule,
source, version, and classification date.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} the label is not a control or state, creates no
\(x_{ig}\), and imposes no response ranking.

\subsubsection{Interface 10: Patent applications}
\textit{Meaning:} candidate patenting-side signal outside the baseline
endogenous outcome. It may reflect upstream research activity after construct
validation, but it is not a direct observation of upstream research.
\textit{Candidate measure:} patent applications or families after assignee
and quality audits.
\textit{Unit:} application/family count or quality-weighted measure.
\textit{Grain:} assignee--patent family--application date.
\textit{Required keys:} assignee, developer crosswalk, family identifier,
application date, and technology class.
\textit{Availability:} NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} a patent response neither equals \(x_i\) nor
directly identifies upstream research, and it does not alone validate or
falsify the commercialization mechanism.

\subsection{Market and outside-option interfaces}
\label{subsec:p10-market-outside}

\subsubsection{Interface 11: CMO price or scarcity \texorpdfstring{\(p_m^*\)}{equilibrium CMO price}}
\textit{Meaning:} equilibrium scarcity of qualified manufacturing service.
\textit{Candidate measure:} service price if observed; otherwise audited
capacity, density, utilization, or waiting-time proxies.
\textit{Unit:} currency per qualified-capacity unit, or a documented proxy
unit.
\textit{Grain:} technology/region--market period.
\textit{Required keys:} technology class, region, period, capacity unit, and
supplier/source identifier.
\textit{Availability:} PRICE DATA NOT VERIFIED; alternative proxies also
require audit.
\textit{Identification limit:} a nonprice scarcity proxy does not separately
identify \(p_m^*\) or the supply and demand schedules.

\subsubsection{Interface 12: Transfer outside option \texorpdfstring{\(T(q,m)\)}{transfer value}}
\textit{Meaning:} non-retained transfer or licence value.
\textit{Candidate measure:} future transfer/licence deal moments.
\textit{Unit:} currency per project or standardized deal-value index.
\textit{Grain:} project--licensor--licensee--deal date.
\textit{Required keys:} project, licensor, licensee, deal date, deal scope,
and disclosed value components.
\textit{Availability:} FUTURE INTERFACE; NOT VERIFIED.
\textit{Identification limit:} the outside option is not endogenized in the
baseline, and incomplete deal values need not identify \(T(q,m)\).

\subsection{Timing interface}
\label{subsec:p10-timing}

The theoretical anticipation channel remains
\begin{equation}
  M
  \longrightarrow
  \text{anticipated availability and value of }E
  \longrightarrow
  \Omega_i
  \longrightarrow
  x_i^*,
  \label{eq:p10-anticipated-interface}
\end{equation}
whereas the realized sequence remains
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
  x_i^*
  &\longrightarrow
  \text{planning-stage projects}
  \longrightarrow r_i^* \\
  &\longrightarrow
  \text{observed holder--producer separation}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{realized products}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:p10-realized-interface}
\end{equation}
Future tests may examine both sequences, but an observed downstream separation
cannot be placed before the ex ante choice of \(x_i\).

\subsection{Interface conclusion}
\label{subsec:p10-conclusion}

These entries are schemas for future validated data. No empirical data set is
asserted to be currently available. Before use, every candidate source must pass provenance,
coverage, key-uniqueness, unit, timing, and construct-validity audits. The
model may discipline composite moments without separately identifying every
primitive.
