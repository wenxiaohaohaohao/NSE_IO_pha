\section*{Phase 14: Assumption Discipline}
The active assumptions are organized in four blocks: demand and commercial
returns; manufacturing technologies; project advancement; and CMO-market
regularity. Architecture guards and derived conclusions are separately
identified. Each proposition records the assumptions actually used.

The realization level enters route value and therefore advancement; only a
direct policy shift of that probability is excluded. Interior derivatives,
endpoint continuity, no-tie and integrable-envelope conditions are explicit.
No desired policy sign, class ranking or patent response is an assumption.
