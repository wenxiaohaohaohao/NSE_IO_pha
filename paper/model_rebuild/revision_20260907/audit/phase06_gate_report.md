## Independent supply rederivation

The supplier objective derivative is p-Psi_s. At p>0 it is positive at zero and negative at sufficiently large s; strict Psi_ss>0 gives one root and a global optimum. At p=0, all positive capacities have positive cost and zero capacity uniquely maximizes. Positive supplier mass makes the integral strictly increasing; compact-price domination gives continuity, and monotone convergence gives unbounded supply. The dimensional identity p*s is currency; Psi_s is currency/capacity.

## Independent demand and closure rederivation

For each fixed project the E value is affine in price with strictly negative slope -b, and every competing route is price-invariant. Its winning set is therefore a lower price interval. Thus chi is nonincreasing. The maximum is Lipschitz in price with bound b; its almost-everywhere derivative is -b times the E indicator. E_F b finite justifies the Omega envelope derivative. Chain differentiation of the Phase 5 optimizer gives x_p=-x chi/(nu Omega) for positive Omega; monotonicity holds at the zero corner without dividing by zero.

For p2>p1, both nonnegative x and chi weakly decline, so x(p2)chi(p2)<=x(p1)chi(p1). Integration preserves this inequality, including individual route switches. At any candidate limit price, no maximizing tie a.s. gives indicator convergence; the explicit global envelope permits dominated convergence over projects and developers. Continuity is proved rather than obtained by smoothing.

Excess demand is continuous, strictly decreasing, strictly positive at zero and negative at sufficiently large price: E vanishes pointwise as p tends to infinity, demand tends to zero by the same envelope, supply grows without bound. IVT gives existence; strict monotonicity gives uniqueness. At the old root, reform excess demand equals nonnegative new study demand; the new root is weakly larger and strictly larger if that study demand is positive. This is an endogenous response, not a policy-assumed price movement.
