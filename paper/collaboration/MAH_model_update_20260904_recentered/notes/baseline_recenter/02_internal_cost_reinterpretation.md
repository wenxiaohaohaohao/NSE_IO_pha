# 02 Internal-Cost Reinterpretation

Status: complete

For every $k_i>0$, internal marginal and setup costs are finite:

\[
0<c_I(m,k_i)<\infty,
\qquad
0\le F_I(m,k_i)<\infty.
\]

They satisfy $c_{I,m}>0$, $c_{I,k}<0$, $F_{I,m}>0$, and $F_{I,k}<0$.
Weak capability therefore represents the high real cost of building,
acquiring, upgrading, validating, or adapting internal production assets. It
does not imply a hard prohibition.

The sorting derivative remains

\[
\partial_k\Delta_{IE}
=s(q)R_c(q,c_I)c_{I,k}-F_{I,k}>0,
\]

because $R_c<0$, $c_{I,k}<0$, and $F_{I,k}<0$. Existence of a cutoff uses a
finite-point crossing condition; an optional continuous $k\downarrow0$ limit
is interpretation only.
