# Finance Baseline versus Recentered Model

| Dimension | Archived finance-baseline version | Revised organizational baseline | Inactive finance extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central question | How financing capacity changes which commercialization route can be funded | How MAH separates authorization ownership from manufacturing and changes commercialization organization | Whether limited pledgeability additionally restricts an otherwise valuable organization |
| Developer type | $(a,k,\ell)$ | $(a,k)$ | $(a,k,\ell)$ only inside the extension |
| Primary heterogeneity | Manufacturing capability plus financing capacity | Internal manufacturing capability $k$ | Financing capacity $\ell$ is secondary |
| Weak $k$ | High internal costs plus a financeability requirement | High but finite $c_I$ and $F_I$ for every $k>0$ | May also raise the extension-only internal liquidity threshold |
| Route choice | Financeability screen followed by value comparison | Ordinary deterministic comparison of $I,E,T,A$ | Finance-adjusted deterministic comparison, if later activated |
| MAH channel | Makes retained entrusted production available; finance constraints determine who can use it | Makes retained entrusted production available through $\tau_E(M)$ | Same policy channel; MAH never increases financing supply |
| Main results | Finance-adjusted sorting, financing corridor, advancement, CMO equilibrium | Organizational sorting, MAH-relevant set, advancement, CMO equilibrium and scarcity | Local financing regions and exact nesting; no main-text proposition |
| CMO aggregation | $H(a,k,\ell)$ | $H(a,k)$ | Would require a separately re-audited finance-adjusted demand system |
| Empirical priority | Capability-finance interaction | Holder--manufacturer separation first; capability heterogeneity second | Pre-policy finance interaction only after capability and firm controls |
| Relation to Gu (2024) | Financing was close to the model's organizing mechanism | Gu's clinical-development/patent evidence is a boundary; this paper explains commercialization organization and retained rights | Financing can be tested as an amplification channel without redefining the paper |
| Patent prediction | None after the patent module was archived | None; patents are not a baseline endogenous outcome | None in this financing extension |

The revised baseline is narrower and more directly tied to the institutional
content of MAH. It explains why a developer with weak internal manufacturing
capability may retain the marketing authorization while purchasing qualified
production services. The financing mechanism is preserved rather than
discarded, but it must earn baseline status through an independent empirical
prediction for holder--manufacturer separation.
