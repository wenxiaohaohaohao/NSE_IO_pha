# Empirical Mapping for Financing Capacity

The object \(\ell_i\) must be measured with validated pre-policy information.
Candidate interfaces include registered or paid-in capital, liquid assets,
leverage or debt capacity, listed status, VC/PE or parent backing, and prior
external-financing history. These are candidate proxies, not claims that a
specific data source is already usable.

The central empirical interaction is

\[
MAH\times\text{weak internal manufacturing capability}
\times\text{limited/intermediate pre-policy financing capacity}.
\]

The preferred outcomes are holder--manufacturer separation, development-stage
project advancement, and retained approvals or realized products. Patent
applications are not a baseline endogenous outcome. The baseline mechanism is
distinct from a research--development allocation model: MAH changes the
capital intensity of the retained commercialization organization but does not
increase financing supply.
