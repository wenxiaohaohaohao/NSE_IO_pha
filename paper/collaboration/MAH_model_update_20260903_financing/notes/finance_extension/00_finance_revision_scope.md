The revision adds one predetermined developer characteristic,
commercialization financing capacity \(\ell_i\), and two liquidity
requirements, \(J_I(m,k_i)\) and \(J_E(m)\). It changes which retained
commercialization organizations are financeable. It does not add a financing
choice, credit-supply shift, capital market, or direct constraint on the
common advancement control \(x_i\).

The direct policy arrow remains \(M\to\tau_E(M)\). In particular,
\(M\nrightarrow\ell_i,J_I,J_E,a_i,k_i,F,s\). The user-requested financing
revision supersedes the formerly active research--development/patent
allocation extension. That source is preserved under
`paper/model_rebuild/archive/` but is not imported by either formal document.

Acceptance boundary: one common \(x_i\), deterministic \(I/E/T/A\) choice,
one scalar CMO market, route-independent downstream realization, no patent
production function, and no welfare, entry, dynamic, or finance-market block.
