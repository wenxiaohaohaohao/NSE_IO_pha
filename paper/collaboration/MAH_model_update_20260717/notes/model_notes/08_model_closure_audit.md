## 2. Required solve chain

\[
(M,\eta,\theta_i)
\rightarrow
\mathcal R_i(M)
\rightarrow
G_i^r
\rightarrow
(P_i,\Gamma_i,x_i^*)\text{ at candidate }p_m
\rightarrow
p_m^*(M,\eta)
\rightarrow
(x_i^*,\lambda_i^{plan,*},\lambda_i^{obs})
\rightarrow
\text{aggregate data moments}.
\]

The CMO block is simultaneous because candidate-cost route choice and effort determine demand, while clearing determines the equilibrium support cost. Observed realization is downstream and does not feed back into baseline support demand.

## 3. Object-status audit

| Status | Objects | Claim rule |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive or maintained | \(a_i,\kappa,k_i,h_i^I,q_i^E,\mu_i^E,S_i,\tau_i^T,\beta,\sigma_r\) | Do not claim approval-side identification |
| Policy or implementation | \(M,\eta,\tau_i^E(\eta)\) | Separate route-set availability from continuous implementation |
| Endogenous baseline | \(p_m^*(M,\eta),P_i,\Gamma_i,x_i^*\) | Must be generated by the stated equations |
| Composite realization | \(\zeta_i^r=s_i\chi_i^r\) | Do not split without additional transition data |
| Calibration-facing | realized holder--producer share, realized counts, CMO capacity or support-cost proxies | Match to aggregate or data-specific equations |
| Out of scope | product-market equilibrium, free entry, exit, invariant distribution, welfare | Add only as explicit extensions |

## 4. Claim audit

A proposed statement fails if it:

1. treats the reform as a continuous change in \(M\);
2. omits \(\eta\) from the equilibrium support-cost comparison;
3. calls \(a_i x_i\) a successful or approved-drug count;
4. treats \(q_i^E\) as a completed match;
5. removes the common-success factorization of retained realization;
6. uses realized output to define planning-stage support demand;
7. treats an aggregate observed share as one firm's route probability;
8. applies the narrow-cell log-odds equation to a heterogeneous aggregate;
9. omits relative realization from that narrow-cell log odds;
10. presents Jia or Barwick as the baseline MAH channel;
11. broadens the baseline beyond the single CMO market closure.

## 5. Remaining limitations, not closure failures

- Approval-side data identify composite route-realization wedges, not every primitive.
- Direct CMO support-cost data may be unavailable and require transparent sensitivity analysis.
- Aggregate shares require integration or simulation over heterogeneity.
- Entry, producer matching, cross-cell substitution, and welfare require additional modules.

Within these declared limits, the baseline model is closed.
