# 12 Calibration Claim Audit

## 1. Audit question

Do current calibration statements map feasible data moments to the model's actual aggregate and timing objects without claiming separate identification of unavailable primitives?

## 2. Completion criteria

A calibration statement passes only if it:

1. separates route-planning arrival from realized approvals or launches;
2. maps holder--producer split to an aggregate realized-weighted ratio;
3. restricts log-odds algebra to a homogeneous or sufficiently narrow two-route \(I\)-\(E\) cell;
4. includes relative realization in that log-odds equation;
5. uses \(p_m^*(M,\eta)\) as the equilibrium cost of qualified support;
6. keeps \(D_m^B\) and planning-stage entrusted demand in CMO closure;
7. treats \(q_i^E\) as eligibility rather than an observed match;
8. states required normalizations and unidentified components;
9. separates the Jia approval-delay alternative from the Barwick demand alternative;
10. keeps entry and welfare outside the baseline claim.

## 3. Valid model-to-data mappings

| Data moment | Model counterpart | Supported interpretation | Unsupported interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holder--producer split among comparable realized retained original drugs | Aggregate realized-weighted \(s_E^{obs}(M,\eta)\) | Composite route attractiveness and realization | A primitive policy friction or a representative-firm route probability |
| Narrow-cell entrusted share | Two-route realized conditional share | Composite payoff gap plus relative realization | Heterogeneous aggregate payoff gap |
| Realized original-drug counts | \(a_i x_i^*\sum_rP_i(r)\zeta_i^r\omega_i^{r,d}\) | Arrival and realization scale jointly | Direct observation of route-planning arrival or scientific productivity |
| CMO capacity, participation, or support-cost proxy | Supply, background demand, or \(p_m^*(M,\eta)\) sensitivity | Strength of support-cost attenuation | Product-market price or demand |
| Post-MAH exposure gradient | Cell-level aggregate share generated by integration or simulation | Reduced-form discipline on implementation heterogeneity | Primitive structural derivative under unrestricted heterogeneity |
| Firm-year research entry | Entry module, if credibly measured | Entry-cost distribution | Baseline identification from product approvals alone |

## 4. Canonical holder--producer moment

\[
s_E^{obs}(M,\eta)
=\frac{\int a_i x_i^*P_i(E)\zeta_i^E\,dH_i}
{\int a_i x_i^*
\sum_{r\in\mathcal R_i^{ret}(M)}
P_i(r)\zeta_i^r\,dH_i}.
\]

Firm heterogeneity, R&D selection, route choice, and realization all enter. The aggregate moment must be computed by integration or simulation.

For a homogeneous or sufficiently narrow two-route cell only,

\[
\log\frac{s_{iE}^{obs}}{1-s_{iE}^{obs}}
=\frac{G_i^E-G_i^I}{\sigma_r}
+\log\frac{\zeta_i^E}{\zeta_i^I}.
\]

The factorization \(\zeta_i^r=s_i\chi_i^r\) allows common success to cancel inside this narrow ratio, not before aggregation.

## 5. Canonical CMO moment

\[
D_m^{tot}(p_m;M,\eta)
=D_m^B(p_m)
+\int a_i x_i^*(M,\eta,p_m)
P_i(E\mid M,\eta,p_m)\,dH_i.
\]

One planned entrusted project reserves one support package. Final realization does not enter this demand equation. Regularity conditions determine a unique \(p_m^*(M,\eta)\), and the IFT supplies the implementation-intensity response.

## 6. Required claim language

Use:

- consistent with the MAH commercialization-route mechanism;
- disciplines a composite route-realization wedge;
- jointly disciplines planning-stage arrival and downstream realization;
- conditional on normalization and support-cost sensitivity;
- aggregate realized-weighted share.

Do not use:

- separately identifies the MAH friction;
- directly measures successful project arrival;
- eligibility proves a producer match;
- aggregate log odds equal a representative-firm payoff difference;
- approval-side data identify entry costs;
- the calibration establishes welfare.

## 7. Alternative-channel controls

- Jia et al. motivate approval-delay, review-capacity, and pipeline-composition controls or alternative explanations.
- Barwick et al. motivate demand, reimbursement, and market-size controls or alternative explanations.
- Neither changes the baseline definition of MAH as a route-availability and route-friction reform with qualified-support scarcity.

## 8. Audit result

Pass, subject to the stated data and normalization limits. The current control standard is deliberately composite: it supports a mechanism calibration, not primitive-by-primitive structural identification.
